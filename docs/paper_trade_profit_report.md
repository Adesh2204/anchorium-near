## Profit target

Otherwise hold, with no stop-loss and no time limit, until the report cut-off (2026-11-14 12:30:00 UTC (18:00 IST)). A position still open at the cut-off is reported open, valued at a final dry quote.

## Method and limits

- **Signals.** The frozen strategy (vendored unchanged; see `vendor/anchorium_strategy/SOURCE.md`) runs on completed 1-minute Hyperliquid perp candles. Execution is spot on NEAR Intents: a LONG buys, and only a held position can be sold.
- **Exit rule.** `PROFIT_TARGET` (see above). It is an execution rule added for this session, not a strategy change: the frozen strategy still chose the entry. Strategy SHORT signals were ignored while holding. The sale is journaled as `PROFIT_EXIT` with source `profit-target`, not as a strategy signal.
- **Fills.** Simulated at the dry quote's `amountOut`. A live swap settles at or above `minAmountOut`, so a real fill could differ from these figures by up to the worst-case slippage shown.
- **Sample size.** One round trip says nothing about the strategy's edge. It shows the pipeline: signal → guard check → quote → on-chain decision → outcome, with real costs.
- **No guarantee.** A profit-target exit makes every *closed* trade profitable by construction, but a trade whose price never recovers stays open at a loss. With no stop, the loss on an open position is not capped.
